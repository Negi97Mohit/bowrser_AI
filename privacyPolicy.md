# Privacy Policy — Page Diff
_Last updated: May 2026_

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Page Diff** Chrome extension handles information. Page Diff helps users monitor webpage changes by saving local snapshots of webpage content and comparing updates over time.

## Data Collection

**Page Diff does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.** No information about you or your browsing activity is ever sent to any server operated by the developer.

## What the Extension Does With Your Data

- **Webpage text content** — accessed solely to generate local snapshots for comparison. It is never transmitted or stored outside your device.
- **Page URLs** — used to identify and track which pages are being monitored. They are never sent to any external server.
- **Tab information** — accessed only as required for snapshot comparison functionality. No tab data leaves your device.

All snapshot and monitoring data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage APIs.

## Third-Party Services

Page Diff does not use external servers or third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising services.

## Permissions Explained

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| `storage` | To save page snapshots locally on your device |
| `tabs` | To access tab information required for snapshot comparison |
| `activeTab` | To access the content of the currently active tab |
| `scripting` | To capture webpage content for snapshot generation |
| `alarms` | To schedule periodic checks for webpage changes |
| `notifications` | To alert you when changes are detected on a monitored page |
